Decision-Weighted D-Optimal Probes for Depth-One QAOA Landscape Recovery

The study selects a small set of angle observations by combining regularized information gain with a development-only optimizer-sensitivity score.

Sixteen of 1024 angle values recover six of eight authenticated Gset grid optimizers. Maximum regret is \(1.734\times10^{-3}\), compared with \(1.997\times10^{-3}\) for unweighted D-optimal selection and a random-design median of \(3.378\times10^{-2}\).

The manuscript proves submodularity and a deterministic noise-and-residual certificate. It also reports the negative runtime control: row-Gram learning is faster than hierarchical action iteration at the evaluated dimensions.
